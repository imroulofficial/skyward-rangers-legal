Privacy Policy — Skyward Rangers

Developer: Red Pen Studio
App: Skyward Rangers (Android package com.redpenstudio.skywardrangers)
Effective date: 24 November 2026
Contact: imroulofficial@gmail.com

1. Local-only game data — no server, no account, no login

Skyward Rangers is an offline-first game. All game data — player level, progress, heroes, buildings, currencies, missions, settings and save backups you choose to export — is stored locally on your phone only. There is no external server, no online account, and no login. We (Red Pen Studio) do not operate a server for this game and do not collect, receive or store your gameplay data on our systems.

If you export a save backup, that file stays on your device (or wherever you choose to save it). Uninstalling the app deletes the locally stored game data.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The game shows ads only when your device is online, using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). When ads are shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, device information and app interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. If you are in the EEA or UK, a consent dialog is shown when online. No ads are shown during active gameplay, and the game works fully offline without ads.

Our app-ads.txt file is published at app-ads.txt.

3. Purchases — Google Play Billing

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Purchase records needed to restore what you bought are handled by Google Play on your device.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not share personal data with anyone, because we do not collect it. The only data leaving your device is what the third-party services above (Google AdMob and Google Play) collect under their own policies when you are online.

5. Audience — 13+

Skyward Rangers is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data security and retention

Because your game data stays on your device, its security depends on your device's own protections. We retain no gameplay data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on request — deleting the app removes the local data. For questions about data handled by Google's services, see Google's privacy controls on your device and in your Google account.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
